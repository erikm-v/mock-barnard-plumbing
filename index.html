What we do

We undertake all of your domestic plumbing needs in the Auckland area. Scroll down and we'll show you how each job goes.

01

Leaks

Dripping tap or burst water pipe

Power bill suddenly increased? Whether it's a dripping tap or a burst water pipe, we have the solution to reduce your bill.

  • Dripping tap
  • Burst water pipe
  1. Spot it The meter keeps running with every tap off. Water is going somewhere it shouldn't.
  2. Isolate Shut the valve. The spray stops and the meter settles.
  3. Cut out the damage The split section comes out. New pipe and brass couplers go in.
  4. Test Water back on. No spray, no puddle, and the dripping tap gets sorted too.

02

Plumbing

Hot Water Cylinder replacements

We deal with all of your domestic plumbing requirements. From upgrades and maintenance to Hot Water Cylinder replacements.

  • Hot Water Cylinders
  • Upgrades
  • Maintenance
  1. Isolate and drain Water off at the valve, then the old cylinder is drained.
  2. Old one out Disconnected, lifted and gone.
  3. New one in The new cylinder goes in on its tray.
  4. Connect it up Cold in, hot out, and the relief valve piped to a safe drain.
  5. Heat it Water on, element on, and the hot water is back.

03

Watermains

New watermain or a burst pipe

We can lay your new watermain or fix a burst pipe. We've got you covered.

  • New watermain
  • Burst pipe
  1. Burst main Water coming up through the footpath is the giveaway.
  2. Shut off and dig Isolate the water, then open the trench along the line of the pipe.
  3. Lay the main Old pipe out, new pipe in, joint by joint.
  4. Connect to the house Brass couplers at the meter box and up the wall to your supply.
  5. Water on Turn it on and check the tap at the other end.

Plumbing

New Builds

A new bathroom suite, a shower, a kitchen. Hot and cold go through the frame before the walls go on.

  • New Builds
  • New bathroom suite
  • Shower
  1. The frame goes up Studs and plates, ready for pipes.
  2. Run the pipes Cold from the main, hot from the cylinder, branched to each room.
  3. Waste and fixtures Waste under the slab, then the shower, basin and kitchen tap.
  4. Pressure test Every line gets tested before anyone closes up a wall.
  5. Switch on Water at every outlet.
